## Context

Describe the concrete problem, constraints and evidence. Distinguish verified facts from assumptions and unanswered questions.

## Decision

State the architectural choice and its scope. A proposed implementation must respect published contracts.

## Reasons

Explain why the choice addresses the problem.

## Consequences

State operational, security, compatibility and maintenance costs, including required validation and limits.

## Alternatives

Record meaningful alternatives and why they were not selected. Omit speculative alternatives that did not affect the decision.

## Revisit / upgrade conditions

Identify evidence or changes that warrant revisiting, the owning reviewer, migration implications and required verification.
